### Entrance animations replay (quickly) each time a section comes back into view (LOCAL, not committed or pushed)
- **Owner request:** the entrance animations ran only once per section; they should run every time the visitor scrolls past a section ("option 2 with limits": a short, light replay, never while the section is still visible).
- **How it works** (`src/lib/useScrollReveal.ts`, new function `playOnScroll`, used by the `useScrollReveal` hook and by the Vision section): the **first** time a section plays in full, as designed. **Every later time** it plays the same timeline sped up to about **0.8 seconds** (never slower than the original). It plays when the section's top reaches the usual point while scrolling down, and again as soon as it becomes visible after having been hidden (from above, from below, or sliding back in from the side). The section is put back to its hidden start **only when nothing of it can be seen on the screen**, so nothing disappears in front of the visitor. Reduced motion is unchanged (no entrance, nothing hidden). `replay: false` makes a section play once, as before.
- **Bug found by the owner the same day, and fixed:** the first version decided "off the screen" from scroll positions (a second ScrollTrigger). The first panel of each sideways scene (**Vision/Mission/Values** and **Ecosystem**) stays on the screen for the whole pan while the page scrolls past its unpinned position, so those two were hidden while still sliding out to the left. Now "off the screen" is decided by an `IntersectionObserver` (where the element really is, sideways too), which also replays them when they slide back in. My first browser test had not covered the two scenes; the new one scrolls through both pans and checks every step.
- **Applies to** the six sections with a one-time entrance: Vision/Mission/Values, Ecosystem, Framework, Campus, News and the CTA band. Already scroll-linked and therefore always replaying: the hero, the sideways scenes (Courage, Journey, the Ecosystem to Alumni pan) and the Campus image drift. Vision's own trigger code was replaced by the shared function (its "page loaded below the section" special case is gone: the section stays hidden until it comes back into view and then plays quickly); will-change is set again at the start of each play.
- **Tests:** the hook's unit tests were updated and extended (19 tests: the trigger, one-off mode, full first play, the quicker replays, never slower, reset only when nothing is visible, no double play, replay when visible again, the page loading with the section already in view, stopping the observer, and a browser without IntersectionObserver). In a real browser on the dev server, with the final version: **Framework, Campus and News**: everything is shown after the first entrance; nothing is hidden while any part is on screen; once fully off screen (above or below) it is hidden again; scrolling up into it replays in about 0.2 to 0.5 s and scrolling down into it again in about 0.1 to 0.5 s; **Vision and Ecosystem (the pinned scenes)**: nothing hidden at any step of the pan (0 hidden-while-visible samples), hidden again only after sliding completely out, replay when they slide back in (0.2 to 0.8 s); with reduced motion nothing is ever hidden. ### Fix: the page broke when resizing in DevTools (pinned scenes kept the old size)
- **Reported by the owner:** with DevTools open, resizing the window (device mode) made the site break: the hero slideshow stayed visible behind later sections (Journey, Ecosystem), because the pinned scenes were still measured for the old size.
- **Cause 1 (the one the owner saw):** GSAP's `ignoreMobileResize` (turned on in `src/lib/gsap.ts` so a phone's address bar sliding in and out does not make the pinned scenes jump) ignores any height-only resize under 25% on ANY touch device, and DevTools device mode is one. Docking DevTools at the bottom is about an 18% change, so the pins were never measured again.
- **Cause 2 (found while testing the first fix):** GSAP sends `refreshInit` BEFORE it unpins the scenes, so `HorizontalScene`'s `fit()` measured the panels inside the old, still-pinned width. One refresh after a big width change could settle on slightly wrong panel scales and pin heights (a second refresh happened to fix it in a normal window, but not with touch emulation).
- **Cause 3:** the panels' own animation tweens are placed on the pan once, as fractions worked out for the stage width at build time, so after a resize they started too early or too late (the last Journey column finished about 30% of a step early).
- **Fixes:** `src/lib/resize.ts` (`refreshOnRealResize`, used by `SmoothScroll`): a resize while the page is NOT being scrolled always measures again, a height-only change within 600 ms of scrolling (an address bar) never does, a width change (rotation) always does; a burst of resize events refreshes once. `HorizontalScene`: after every refresh it measures the panels again in the real layout and refreshes once more if the scales differ, and builds the scene again when the stage width changed (all capped, so nothing can loop).
- **Verified, not assumed:** a resized page now has exactly the pin positions and heights of a fresh load at the final size (mouse and touch emulation, three resize patterns), the scene animations are at the same positions as after a fresh load, the hero never shows once its pin is over (31 positions walked), and the phone behaviour is kept (address bar while scrolling ignored, same change while standing still measured, rotation measured). 9 unit tests for the watcher (80 in all); the browser checks are in `npm run qa:interact`. The earlier resize test only looked for errors and overflow, which is why it missed this.
